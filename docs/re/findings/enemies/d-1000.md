# D-1000 (stage 02, main object type 48): behaviour sheet

D-1000 is the intro boss. The fight has two objects of type 48:

- **the body** (`var` 0, record 56): the large walking mechaniloid;
- **the core** (`var` 1, made by the body): a small floating object at one side of the room. It
  shoots at X, and hitting it with a charged shot stuns the body and takes 8 HP from it.

Projectiles and effects are visual objects of type 1, with six variants (`var` 0–5).

Overlay `02` (intro). Read by hand from the disassembly (before the RE kit). Units, confidence tags
and the shared engine rules: `docs/engine.md`. The boss room (start position, walls, wall templates,
core positions): `docs/stages/intro.md`.

## Body: numbers [certain]

- **HP 32** (the boss HP bar maximum, `0x800CCF5C`). Contact damage **6**.
- Contact box and hurt box (−56, −64, 112, 92): X − 56 … X + 56, Y − 64 … Y + 28 (7 × 5.75 blocks).
- Standing height: origin at y 304. Crouched (after landing, during the crouch attack and the stun):
  about y 352, on the floor.
- Damage table `dmg_tbl_d1000` (`0x80074B5C`, in full in `docs/engine.md`). The main values:

  | weapon id | probable weapon | effect on the body |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | X-Buster lemon | **deflected** |
  | 9 | half charge | **deflected** |
  | 18 / 19 / 20 | full charge | 5 / 1 / 4 |
  | 22 | ? | 8 |
  | 24–31 | sabre | 2, 2, 3, 2, 2, 2, 1, 2 |
  | 7, 55 | ? | no effect |
  | 21, 59–62 | ? | deflected |

- After a hit: **30 frames of invincibility** (2 frames after weapon ids 24 and 25, the first sabre
  hits, so a sabre combo connects). During that time the body flashes every 8 frames and uses
  `dmg_tbl_deflect` (all shots bounce off).

## Body: the fight

### Entry (once) [certain for the code; frame counts certain unless marked]

**Trigger.** There is no trigger object. The spawner makes the body from record 56 (6162, 346)
when the record comes into the spawn band during the pass through boss gate 19 (camera x about 5795
or more, while the camera moves from 5744 to 6064) [likely for the moment]. The set-up `D1000Init`
(`0x800EFF4C`) puts the body at (6288, 160), not drawn, with HP 0, contact damage 6, `game+0x20` =
body, `game+0x25` = 0, `boss_hp_max` = 32, `BossLevel`, and calls `PlayerEvent(20, 64)`. X is still
in gate step 19, which takes no requests. At the end of the pass, step 19 calls `Stop` (`0x8003ADB4`),
`Stop` calls `PlayerRequests`, and X goes to step 20 (hold, facing right). `PlayerEvent` also sets
`mega+0x7A` (`no_hit`) = 1 and `game.player_lock` = 1, so X cannot be hurt and the pause menu cannot
open until the end of the HP fill.

The entry is body step 2 (`D1000Entry`, `0x800F0060`; sub-steps `0x800FB1D0`):

Boss gate 19 sets the camera limits (x 6064–6144, y 224). The entry does not change them; it sets
only the ease speed `cam+0x47` = 2.

### THINK (step 4)

Faces X. After **60 frames** it picks the next action. `r` = a random number 0–15:

| situation | HP > 16 | HP ≤ 16 |
|---|---|---|
| just after a CROUCH | r < 8: WALK; else SUMMON | r < 6: SUMMON; else BOMB |
| any other time | r < 8: RAM; else SUMMON | r < 6: RAM; else BOMB |

So above half HP it does not use BOMB; below half HP it never walks straight out of THINK.

### WALK (step 5)

- Faces X and walks toward it at **1 px/frame**.
- If `|X.x − body.x| ≤ 60` px: stop and CROUCH.
- After 120 frames without that: stop, THINK.

### CROUCH (step 6) — a crouching body slam [certain for the motion; the purpose is likely]

1. Drops 48 px (gravity 0.5 px/frame², about 14 frames) to y 352. Sound and rumble.
2. Shakes in place (moves ±2 px every other frame) during its animation and up to frame 60.
3. At frame 90, **or as soon as it has touched X**, it rises 1 px/frame back to y 304 (48 frames).
4. THINK (marked "just after a CROUCH").

The body hurts by contact in all phases; the crouch brings the contact box 48 px lower.

### BOMB (step 7, only at HP ≤ 16)

- Faces X. At frame 24 (Hard: frames 24 and 84) it throws a bomb (sound bank 2, id 6). Ends at
  frame 160 (Hard: 220), then WALK.
- The bomb (visual `var` 1): starts 56 px in front of the body and 24 px above its origin.
  Horizontal speed `(X.x − bomb.x) / 32` px/frame, so it would reach X's x in 32 frames; gravity
  0.25 px/frame². It hurts by contact (6) while it flies.
- When it lands: sound, rumble, and a **fire pillar** erupts at that spot: 32 px wide and 255 px
  tall (from 128 px above to 127 px below its origin, which is 78 px above the landing point), damage
  6, for **50 frames**, then it fades.

### SUMMON (step 8)

- The body stands still, facing X, and waits for the core's signal.
- The core fires **4 aimed shots, 8 frames apart** (frames 0, 8, 16, 24), and at frame 124 it tells
  the body to go on. (Playing as Zero: 3 shots at frames 0, 60, 120; signal at 220.) Then WALK.
- Core shot (visual `var` 2): aimed at X when fired, **2 px/frame**, straight, 16 × 16 px, damage
  **4**. Any player shot destroys it. It bursts on walls.

### RAM (step 9)

1. Wind-up, **180 frames**: faces X, and moves its height toward X's height at 2 px/frame.
2. Charges in its facing direction: acceleration 1 px/frame², top speed **4 px/frame**.
3. Hits the wall: sound, rumble, screen shake, and the wall column is replaced with the dented one.
4. Stays at the wall for **180 frames**. Then the wall is restored, and it rises 1 px/frame to
   y 304. WALK.

### STUN (step 10) — after the core is hit

- Starts at once, from any step. The body loses its contact box (it cannot hurt X) and uses
  `dmg_tbl_deflect` for the whole stun.
- The walls are restored. It staggers (animation), falls to the floor with gravity
  0.375 px/frame², lands (sound, rumble, shake 60, 4, 2).
- **65 frames after landing: HP − 8.** If HP ≤ 0: death.
- Otherwise it stays down until the core sends its signal: the core plays its hit animation, waits
  60 frames, moves to the other side, plays its appear animation and counts 100 frames. So a stun
  lasts at least about 160 frames (≈ 2.7 s) plus the animations. Then the body stands up, rises to
  y 304, gets its contact box and normal damage table back, and goes to THINK.

### Death [certain for the code; times in updates, ± 1–2]

**Kill.** There are two ways. Both set state 2, step 0, keep the HP bar value, clear the flash bit,
and set `d1000_dead` (`0x800FBC70`) = 1 and `mega+0x7A` (`no_hit`) = 1:

- a weapon kill in the main routine (`0x800EDD84`, `ContactWeapon` < 0);
- the stun: 65 updates after the landing, HP − 8 ≤ 0 (`D1000StunHp`, `0x800EE71C`; HP set to 0).

At `d1000_dead`, every visual type 1 object (shots, bomb, pillar, particles) deletes itself, and the
core plays sound (2, 20) and deletes itself. From the kill on, X cannot be hurt. No stop flag is
set; X is held only by the event steps.

**White screen** (step 2, once). Animation 11 (set 1, sprite 44: the wreck). Body y = camera y +
160 (384). `game+0x24` = 0 and `game+0x20` = 0 (no HP bar).

- When body x > camera min x + 200 (6264): X x = body x − 160, `PlayerEvent(20, 64)` (X faces right),
  the body faces left, camera target T = X x − 48.
- Else: X x = body x + 160, `PlayerEvent(20, 0)`, the body faces right, T = X x − 256.

Both target x limits are set to T, clamped to the old target limits (6064–6144). The camera eases
there at 2 px/update while the screen is white. X y does not change.

**After the body.** D-1000 has no defeated bit, no soul and no drop. Nothing in the sequence writes
`game.exit_req`, the checkpoint or the game mode. Zero opens text 3 when the body is deleted, and
then makes High Max (`docs/stages/intro.md`, "Events"). From the kill to text 3: about 1550 updates
(26 s) [likely].

**A death during the fight** [certain for the code]. `StageExit` (`0x8001E634`) takes a life and
goes to game mode 9 (`StageStart`, no stage reload): X respawns at checkpoint 2 (5904, 364), the
records are reset, the Hunters' scene stays skipped (checkpoint 2), and the stage music restarts.
After gate 19 the full entry runs again. The set-up clears `0x800FBC70` and `0x800FBC74`. The boss
pack is not loaded again (`boss_pack_loaded` is cleared only by `StageStartReset`) [likely]. The
room's wall cells changed by `CopyTiles` are not reloaded [likely].

## The core (`var` 1) [certain]

- Made by the body at the end of the entry, on the side of the room away from X (positions in
  `docs/stages/intro.md`). No contact damage. Hurt box (−10, −10, 20, 20): 1.25 × 1.25 blocks. Its HP
  is set to 127 every frame, so it never dies.
- It does not move. Every 6 frames it emits an energy particle that flies to the body's head (a
  visual effect, no damage).
- **Weak point.** While it is active (after the first THINK), a hit with weapon ids 18–21 (full
  charge shots) or 50–51 stuns the body (see STUN). As Zero, any weapon that hits it stuns the body.
  Lemon and half-charge shots do nothing to it.
- After a stun hit: hit animation, then 60 frames later it moves to the other side of the room,
  plays its appear animation, and 100 frames later signals the body to stand up. It becomes a weak
  point again at the body's next THINK.
- When the body dies, the core plays a sound and is removed.

## Sprites [certain]

Type 48 has two sprite sets, with one animation table (`0x800FB138`, 24 animations). Each
`SetAnime` call of overlay 02 first sets the sprite data (`+0x3C`) to `bank_base[k]` or
`bank_base[k + 1]` (`k` = `GetSpriteBank(48)`, 15 in `intro-3_boss`):

- set 0 (entry 15, 16 sprites): animations 0–5, the body;
- set 1 (entry 16, 49 sprites): animations 6–12 and 14–23: the body's moves 6–9 and 11, the core
  (12, 15, 16) and the visual type 1 effects (10, 14, 17–23). No call sets animation 13; it
  uses sprite 16, which only set 1 has.

Texture page `0x1E0` and palette `0x794C` for both sets. So the 49 sprites of entry 16
(`d-1000-extra`) belong to D-1000 type 48 itself (set 1), not to the parts only.

The export of `intro-3_boss` with the `sets` rule (stage 0, type 48, set 1, animations 6–23,
texture `null`, palette 0) is one folder, `enemy-f15c898594` (24 animations, cell 216 × 229),
from the body. The core is skipped as "sprite data of set 1 (entry 16)". The earlier folders stay:
`d-1000` (`enemy-5473b61b53`, 10 animations from entry 15: strips 6–9 are wrong) and `d-1000-parts`
(`enemy-b0ca9371b7`, 24 animations from entry 16: strips 0–5 are wrong). `d-1000-extra`
(`enemy-250854b686`) is the sheet of entry 16. All 16 + 49 sprites of the two sets are in
these exports.

## Damage dealt

Body 6, bomb and fire pillar 6, core shot 4.

## Fight summary for a reimplementation

- Boss with 32 HP. Buster lemons and half charges bounce off the body. Full charges do 5 (with
  30-frame invincibility), sabre 2–3 per hit.
- **Main way to win:** shoot the core (a small target at the far side of the room) with a full
  charge. Each core hit stuns the boss and takes 8 HP: 4 core hits win.
- Moves: walk toward X (1 px/frame) into a crouching body slam; wall ram (3 s wind-up, 4 px/frame
  charge, 3 s stuck at the wall); summon (the core fires 4 aimed 2 px/frame shots); below half HP:
  a lobbed bomb that becomes a 50-frame fire pillar (2 bombs on Hard).
- Contact damage 6, bomb and pillar 6, core shot 4.
